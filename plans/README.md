## Context

Both plans target the bundled-model load intro in `main.js`
(`loadBundledDefaultModel`), which was iteratively hand-tuned over a long
session into three independent tween systems (pitch, phase-1 zoom, phase-2
pan+zoom, plus a separate yaw settle) sharing a set of `MODEL_LOAD_*`
constants.

Plan 001: the two phases read as visibly separate motions with dead time
between them, rather than one continuous camera move. Diagnosis found two
literal stop-then-restart gaps (not just a "feel" issue) caused by
`MODEL_LOAD_PHASE_OVERLAP_MS` under-cancelling `MODEL_LOAD_INTRO_PHASE_GAP_MS`'s
deliberate pause. Fixed with a single constant change.

Plan 002: after 001 closed the literal gap, the zoom specifically (as
opposed to pan/rotation) still doesn't read as continuous — because it's
the *product* of two separately-tweened variables (`cubeSizePercent` ×
`cameraTargetZoomCurrent`) that hand off at a shared zero-velocity instant,
even though there's no longer any dead time between them. Fixed by
overlapping phase-2's zoom start with phase-1's zoom tail and switching
phase-2's zoom to an ease-out curve, so neither side of the handoff is ever
at rest. See the plan for the derivative-level justification.
